<h2>§05b The come-back signal, bolstered v0.42.0</h2>
<div class="note"><strong>§5.27.9 reachable-hours deferral.</strong> A non-safety-critical signal
blocked by silent hours or a blocked window is now deferred to the next
reachable window — capped at one, stale after an hour — instead of silently
dropped. Mirrors the same graceful-degradation philosophy as the quality ladder
(§5.28) and a live game's async fallback. The sender still sees nothing — a
deferral is not a delivery receipt.</div>
