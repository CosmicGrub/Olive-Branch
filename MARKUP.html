§01 What this document is

The rendered product surface, annotated by the decisions that produced it. Settled choices are tagged green; prohibitions red. This document amends in lockstep with the CHANGELOG — enforced by check-markup.mjs (C1–C7) inside npm run verify, not by discipline.

Reconstruction note (0.38.0). The prior MARKUP was never uploaded to the canonical Drive folder and did not survive its container. This document is rebuilt from the demo manifest (which D3 forces to mirror the screen list — all 68 slugs recovered exactly) and the CHANGELOG. Per-screen data-since values are derived from the entries that shipped each surface and are best-effort where the record is coarse.

§02 Standing decisions and prohibitions

Preservation is a standing rule; expiry is a digest, not a default. §10.1b
The capability budget is binding, not advisory — and the voice is never a candidate for shedding. §8.14
Quick to shed, slow to restore: video quality walks down one step at a time, and restoring is six times slower than dropping.
The demo targets the Galaxy Z Fold 5 — both viewports, cover and main, because the device is two devices.
P1 no synthetic parent voice or likeness · P2 no scores or streaks shown to her · P3 no live location · P4 no purchase mechanics · P5 no ads, brokerage, or training on her data · P6 no financial surface at a child role · P7 no parent path to her journal · P8 no editing the parent↔parent log · P9 no unsolicited resurfacing of the pre-separation archive.

§05b The come-back signal, bolstered v0.42.0

§5.27.9 reachable-hours deferral. A non-safety-critical signal blocked by silent hours or a blocked window is now deferred to the next reachable window — capped at one, stale after an hour — instead of silently dropped. Mirrors the same graceful-degradation philosophy as the quality ladder (§5.28) and a live game's async fallback. The sender still sees nothing — a deferral is not a delivery receipt.
