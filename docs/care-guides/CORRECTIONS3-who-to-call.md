## Applied

- R1 Severe bleeding: no source puts the call before pressing (SJA: pressure first, call if you have not already; NHS: call if severe). Now: press firmly at once, shout for someone to call, or if alone call on speaker while pressing (SJA primary survey: shout for a helper or put the phone on speaker and call yourself). Soaked cloth: "put more on top" (NHS cuts page); the NHS accident page says replace it. The difference is in a new disagreement row.
- R2 Burns: healthdirect burns page lists cooling under "what to do while waiting for an ambulance", so the carried order is call (speaker, or someone else), then cool while waiting. AAP raw: "For burns on the face, apply a cold wet washcloth"; healthdirect raw: no cling film on a face burn. One call list (palm-sized or bigger, deep, face, hands, genitals, bottom, electrical, chemical, charred/leathery/white/brown/black/numb, breathing, smoke, hard to wake, unsure) now identical in sections 1, 4 and 7. AAP's feet/joint/hand-sized blistering stays as "seen immediately by a paediatrician or in an emergency room".
- R3 Anaphylaxis: one order (epinephrine first if the child has it, then call at once; with none, call at once, per MedlinePlus "Call 911 ... immediately" and NHS "Call 999 if you think ..."). Second dose trigger: symptoms that get worse or do not improve within 5 minutes (AAP raw: "get worse, continue, or do not get better in 5 minutes"). No doses. Commentary about which authority orders what moved to the disagreement row.
- R4 "cannot speak" became "trouble speaking" (AAP raw). R5 is the same item.
- R6 Top question rewritten: AHA, SJA, Resuscitation Council UK say call on speaker first, American Red Cross lists the call first, BRC and MedlinePlus do not mention a phone.

## Declined or not carried

- R7 "try again at once" for a failed poison-line connection with a well-looking child: poison.org, America's Poison Centers and MedlinePlus say nothing about a failed connection, so no rule was invented. The advice keeps only the sourced 911 rule (unwell, or you believe the child is in danger). Flagged in unverified and clinician question 3.
- R12 Lone adult, no phone, suspected spinal injury: no source; flagged as a clinician question, no wording invented.
- MedlinePlus "do not place a severe burn in cold water" contradicts the cooling rule from NHS, healthdirect and AAP. Cooling is carried (three authorities) and the conflict is a disagreement row and clinician question, not silently dropped.
