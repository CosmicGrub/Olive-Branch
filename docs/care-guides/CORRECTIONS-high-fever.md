# Corrections log: high-fever (revision 2)

Each verifier correction (VERIFY-high-fever.md) was re-checked against the cited page, re-opened on 2026-11-14. Fetched text passes through a summarising model, so meaning and numbers were checked, not exact wording.

## Declined or partly declined

- C8 (rectal depth "about 2.5 cm (1 inch)" is unsupported): NOT confirmed. Two separate fetches of the Canadian Paediatric Society page (Fever and temperature taking) returned a rectal instruction giving about 2.5 cm (1 inch), and a quoted oral instruction of about 1 minute until the beep. The AAP baby page and NHS pages do not state a depth (confirmed). Kept the figure, attributed to CPS only, with "only if shown how" and "follow your thermometer's instructions". The verifier's fetch evidently missed it. Flagged for the clinician in the guide.

## Confirmed and applied

C1, C2, C3, C4, C5, C6, C7, C9b, C9d, C9e, C9f, C9g, C9h, C10, N7 retag, and the missing items (swallowing or drooling, chest pain, new bruises, NHS seizure add-ons, "not eating or not themselves", pharmacist for dose under 2 years, ibuprofen avoided when dehydrated).

Details found while re-checking:
- Swallowing: Cleveland says ER or 911 for excessive drooling or difficulty swallowing; AAP KidsDoc says call doctor or seek care now for serious trouble swallowing fluids or saliva (drooling not listed). Placed on the emergency list (most cautious).
- Chest pain: only the Mayo content lists it (immediate care). Not in KidsDoc, NHS or MedlinePlus.
- New bruises: MedlinePlus 911 list. Painful urination is "immediate care" in Mayo content but "seek care now" in KidsDoc; kept in the urgent same-day list with that note.
- Seizure: AAP KidsDoc = ER now; MedlinePlus = 911; NHS = 999 for first, over 5 min, breathing, one-sided, sleepy over an hour, more than one in 24 hours; CPS = 911 over 3 minutes or under 6 months, exam within hours; RCH = 000 first, over 5 min, not waking; AAP Febrile Seizures = 5 min; AAP baby page = 15 min; Mayo content = over 5 min or slow recovery. Guide follows the most cautious.
- Hot car: AAP When to Call says call right away for fever after a very hot place; KidsDoc mentions overheating only as a cause of mild fever; AAP baby page uses 105 F for heatstroke.
- Duration: guide now says under 2 years over 24 h; 2 and over not improving after 2 days (RCH; CPS 48-72 h). AAP 3 days and NHS 5 days recorded as less cautious.
- 3-6 months: guide follows "any fever, contact the same day". KidsDoc says within 24 hours; NHS 39 C and AAP baby page 101 F are less cautious.
- Cleveland does not mention a week-long recurrence; that is MedlinePlus (fixed).
- Cleveland and CPS do not say "do not undress"; NHS (and CPS: do not strip completely; Seattle: avoid too many layers) do (retagged).
- AAP When to Call lists "has had a seizure", immune problems, heart problems and "looks very ill" under call right away. The guide keeps these in the same-day list with a "do not wait until tomorrow" note, apart from seizure (emergency list). A clinician may prefer to promote them.
