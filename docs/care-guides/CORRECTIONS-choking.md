# Corrections log: choking guide, revision 2

Each verifier correction (VERIFY-choking.md) was re-checked against raw page text (curl plus HTML strip) or a verbatim fetch of the cited page, 2026-11-14.

## Applied

- C1 honey/battery: confirmed (poison.org: honey with no age limit; Alder Hey: honey only over 12 months, nothing else to eat or drink). Reworded to the verifier's text with emergency-now wording.
- C2 15-minute cough: confirmed in raw AAP text ("whether the object is coughed out or not"). Qualifier restored.
- C3 call if unsure: UMass Memorial Health says call 911 even with a strong cough (confirmed raw). Carried into step 1.
- C4 infant hand position: confirmed raw. Red Cross, NHS: two fingers. MedlinePlus: heel of one hand. AHA newsroom: heel of one hand. Also found St John NSW "two fingers on the lower half of the sternum" and Healthy WA "two fingers in the CPR compression position". Both shown, disagreement entry updated, St John NSW attribution fixed. Depth only from Red Cross.
- C5 mouth check: confirmed. MedlinePlus (do not grab if alert, though the same page also says remove if seen) and UMass Memorial Health (do not put a finger in the mouth) vs NHS and St John UK. Added as a new disagreement entry; step carries the no-finger reading; "check the mouth" changed to "look".
- C6 magnets: partly applied. Alder Hey wording re-read. A US federal page (CPSC) was read and says only "seek immediate medical attention". The verifier's "ER or 911, Poison Control on the way" has no US source, so Poison Control for magnets was NOT added. US wording flagged clinician-to-confirm.
- C7 car seat: CDC raw text says "avoid letting your child eat in the car or stroller". Fixed.
- C8 call-first: St John NSW says "Immediately call Triple Zero"; Healthy WA lists calling as step one. The guide keeps source 8 and adds source 7.
- C9 lone rescuer: phone-on-speaker is supported by NHS and UMass Memorial Health. No-phone timing shown as 1 vs 2 minutes.
- Extra, found during re-check: sources 19 and 20 (ummhealth.org) are UMass Memorial Health, not the University of Maryland. Corrected everywhere (the verifier repeated the error).
- Extra: Red Cross says "get equipment", not "get an AED". Corrected.

## Declined

- C7 part: remove "medicine syringes". Declined. The raw AAP page lists "Medicine syringes" under household choking hazards. The verifier's claim was wrong.
- AED mention: declined. Only UMass Memorial Health explicitly says to get an AED; the Red Cross choking pages say only "get equipment". Needs two authorities.
- "Healthy WA page 9 is St John WA content": not checked, noted as unverified.
- The phrase "any recognised method is better than none": no source read states it, so it was not used. The guide says "authorities differ; follow the method taught in your first-aid course".

## Still uncertain

Child sequence (back blows then abdominal vs chest thrusts), infant hand position, call timing, CPR breaths-first vs compressions-first, honey, US magnet wording, AED. A paediatric clinician must resolve these before any in-app use.
