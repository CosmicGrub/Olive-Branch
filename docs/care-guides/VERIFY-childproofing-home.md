## 2. Corrections needed (exact replacement wording)

1. **Bed sharing item (nursery).** Replace with: "Do not bed share. The AAP says to avoid it under any circumstances; room sharing without bed sharing is safest. Never fall asleep with baby on a sofa or armchair." (AAP [0], NICHD [11].) Move the doze-while-feeding steps into the clinician question, not the advice list, per the guide's own note. Keep NHS/Health Canada steps in disagreements only.
2. **Incline.** Replace "Nothing inclined more than 10 degrees" with "Flat and level, never inclined. Avoid inclined sleepers, hammocks, crib wedges and nursing pillows or other products not made for infant sleep." (CDC [1], NICHD [11], Health Canada [32], AAP [0].)
3. **Window opening.** Replace the 4-inch-only wording with: "Limit openings to no more than 4 inches (10 cm) (CPSC, Safe Kids, CPS). UK NHS advises under 6.5 cm (2.5 in). Where the tighter limit still leaves an adult escape route, use it." Delete age band "6m-8y". Update the disagreement note, which currently says "Carry 4 in".
4. **988 row.** Replace with: "988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988 (US)." Delete "text TALK to 741-741" unless verified directly at crisistextline.org; independent sources give keyword HOME.
5. **Suicide-prevention item.** Replace "ask a clinician about door locks" with "you may need to disable locks on bathroom and bedroom doors (AAP [28])". Delete "ages 10-17y"; use "any child or teen".
6. **Delete or source:** "Food is the leading choking cause" (K9); "not with an older sibling in charge" (K11); "fix carpet firmly" (F9); walker "6-18m" and "may delay walking" (S15); "brim visible", scooter helmets (O5); "power covers", "remove toys from the pool" (W3); "bittering agent" (K19); swaddle rationale (S10); "talk early and often about drugs and alcohol" (B5); "keep TVs off dressers, remotes and toys off furniture" (F2).
7. **Citation fixes.** Quebec number: cite [13]. Hiding-guns line: cite [4]. Chemicals in food/drink containers: cite [21] only. 3-12m magnets: cite [10] not [3,9,18]. Empty buckets: add [5].
8. **Honey entry (disagreements).** Replace "while heading to the emergency room" with "after calling 911 and while waiting for care", or remove the dose figures entirely. Keep the ship decision: advice list says only "call 911 / go to the emergency department now; call the National Battery Ingestion Hotline 800-498-8666".
9. **Unverified list.** The bedside-sleeper bullet says the AAP page calls them acceptable; the AAP safe sleep page does not mention bedside sleepers (the CPSC page does). Reattribute to CPSC [2].
10. **Australia 13 11 26.** Add healthdirect.gov.au/poisoning as a source, say "24 hours from anywhere in Australia; call 000 in an emergency", remove the "confirm national routing" caveat.
11. **Magnets item.** Reword: "Keep all loose magnets, especially small high-powered ones and magnetic toys with small pieces, away from children; seek urgent medical care if one or more may have been swallowed." Cite NHS [10] and CPSC (to be added by opening cpsc.gov).
12. **Generators.** Add "more than 20 feet from windows, doors and vents" (CDC [8]).

## 3. Missing items authorities agree belong

Authorities cover these; the draft omits or under-describes them.

- Inclined sleepers, nursing pillows, loungers named as products to avoid (AAP, Health Canada).
- Baby carrier/sling safety (NHS: face visible, do not feed in a sling; AAP: move sleeping baby to flat surface, already included once).
- Offer a pacifier at nap/bedtime (CDC; NHS "consider a dummy"); no hats or head coverings indoors (AAP, CDC).
- Magnet ingestion severity and what to do (CPSC/NHS).
- Button batteries also inhaled or inserted; hotline plus ED immediately; give nothing else to eat or drink (AAP [9]).
- Crisis lines outside the US: the guide lists US-only suicide help; UK/Canada/Australia equivalents need verified official numbers before localising.
- Houseplants and poisons (AAP names it; draft lists it only as unverified).
- Bath water temperature figure (draft correctly says not found; no authority fetched gave one).

Safe sleep ABCs, tip-over, arm's-reach water, 120F water, medicine/cleaner/laundry-packet storage, button batteries, window guards, cords, CO/smoke alarms, hot cars and locked-unloaded firearm storage are all present. Walkers, bumpers, positioners, weighted items, soft bedding and bed sharing are addressed. Dosing: only the honey figure, kept out of advice.

## 7. Overall verdict

**NEEDS CORRECTIONS FIRST.**

No advice is unsafe in the NOT SAFE sense, and the highest-risk topics (sleep, firearms, water, batteries) are broadly aligned with AAP/CDC/CPSC. But two places are less cautious than the authorities (10-degree incline wording; 4 in chosen over NHS 6.5 cm against the stated most-cautious rule), bed sharing is worded softer than the AAP and includes harm-reduction steps contrary to the guide's own note, the 988/741-741 line is garbled, and about a dozen small claims or age bands are not on the cited pages. Fix items 1-12, then it can go to the clinician.
