# CORRECTIONS: head-injury (revision 2)

Date: 2026-11-14. Each verifier correction (C1 to C9) was re-checked against pages re-opened this session (Cleveland Clinic, RCH fact sheet and clinician guide, healthdirect, CPS, MedlinePlus, Seattle Children's, AAP head injury and AAP sports, CDC signs, CDC abusive head trauma, NHS, Pregnancy Birth and Baby, SickKids, ACEP, KidsHealth). Rows of the verifier table not touched by a correction rely on the verifier's own verdicts and were not independently re-read.

## Applied

- C1 vomiting: confirmed. Cleveland (911), CDC (repeated vomiting, 911 or ED), RCH (hospital if more than once, 000 if cannot stop), healthdirect, CPS (hospital right away), MedlinePlus (medical help right away), PBB. Moved to the emergency tier. A single vomit became "call doctor or nurse line now" (MedlinePlus, PBB). Seattle says call the doctor at 2 or more; ACEP says more than once or twice in an hour; AAP 2 to 3 stays as the lenient position in disagreements. The tier heading now says "or go to the emergency department" because most of these sources say hospital, not literally 911.
- C2 tiers: confirmed against Seattle. New seekMedicalHelpNow tier holds under-1, split or gaping cut, bleeding after 10 minutes of pressure, swelling over 2.5 cm, large dent, hard object, dangerous height, cut with no tetanus shot ever. The 5-year and 10-year tetanus rules and headache over 24 hours stay in the 24-hour list. The verifier's phrase "within the hour" was not applied: Seattle says "now" and nothing opened says "within the hour".
- C3 medicine: confirmed (AAP acetaminophen unless doctor agrees and says nothing of ibuprofen or aspirin; Seattle allows ibuprofen after 2 hours without vomiting and bans aspirin; NHS and healthdirect mention paracetamol). Aspirin ban attributed to Seattle only. Poison Help 1-800-222-1222 added from MedlinePlus (an NIH page). The official poison-help sites returned 403, and MedlinePlus gives no hours, so no hours are stated.
- C4 citations: E7 now [1],[7],[2],[0]; E15 adds [9] and [2]; D12 marked general practice with no source; concussion same-day uses SickKids (AAP only says "as soon as possible"); sport rules cite CPS plus AAP written release; skiing and skating cite CPS only; under-3 rate cites PBB only.
- C5: AAP page exclusions (under 2, bleeding disorders, seizure or movement disorders, developmental delay, neck injury, abuse) added to step 7.
- C6 sleep: applied as sleep allowed, no source says keep awake, sources differ, cannot-wake means emergency.
- C7 non-accidental injury: added a separate non-accusing section from the RCH clinician guide (consider abuse in all head injuries; consider it when history does not explain the injury; possible abuse is a reason not to discharge) and CDC (under 1 greatest risk; bleeding around the brain or behind the eyes; calming steps). One sentence asking carers to give an honest account is flagged as general practice. CDC-INFO 800-232-4636 added. No Childhelp number (CDC page prints none).
- C8 missing signs: pallor over an hour, looks sicker, persistent ringing, recurring dizziness (AAP); severe headache or stiff neck (MedlinePlus); blood thinners (NHS 111) and bleeding disorder (RCH clinician guide, AAP exclusion).
- C9 disagreements: Seattle noted as the only time-rule source; CPS, CDC, PBB, ACEP added to vomiting; healthdirect wording fixed; Seattle wake-once marked optional; pain-medicine entry corrected.
- Item 7 rewording: shake/pick-up, stuck object/washing, soaked-cloth, and temper/violence lines reworded.

- C4 "furniture away from windows needs a source": KidsHealth does state it. Kept.
- D6 "do not delay medical care for a baby": no opened source states it; not added.
- P7 (poison control number) via official poison-help sites: not possible (403); used MedlinePlus instead.
- Verifier Section 6 extras not added: alcohol/drug avoidance for 48 h (only the NHS and MedlinePlus lines already present for teens), graded return to school detail, car seats.
