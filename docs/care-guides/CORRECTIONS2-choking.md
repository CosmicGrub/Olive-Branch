# CORRECTIONS2: choking guide, revision 2 to revision 3

Every item below was re-checked against raw page text (curl plus text extract) before being applied. CDC and CPSC block curl, so those two rest on WebFetch quotes. Replacement wording was written fresh (paraphrased), not copied from VERIFY2.

## Applied

- R1 structure: steps are now one sequence per group (first steps, baby, child, child you cannot reach around or pregnant teen, unresponsive, after). Authority disagreements (hand position, call timing, breaths vs compressions, lone-rescuer timing, mouth check, child sequence) are in Disagreements; steps carry only "authorities differ; follow the method taught in your first-aid course". Baby chest thrusts keep two fingers (Red Cross, NHS, SickKids, St John NSW, Healthy WA all raw-confirmed); heel of one hand (MedlinePlus, AHA 2025) is in Disagreements.
- R2 baby CPR: both techniques disclosed and marked needs clinician or instructor (NHS 18 raw: tips of 2 fingers, 4 cm; Red Cross CPR page 23 raw: both thumbs encircling, about 1.5 in, one hand if depth not reached). The VERIFY2 wording "just below the nipple line" for baby CPR was NOT used in the step because the NHS CPR page does not say it; the step says "breastbone, centre of the chest".
- R3 honey: doses removed from every parent-facing place, kept only in clinicianOnly (display false) and its md section; never honey under 12 months; call the hotline.
- R4 Canada: SickKids (11) raw says "call 911"; helpline and text fixed, unverified mislabel removed, first-person wording removed.
- R5 AED: confirmed raw in UMass 19 (find an AED, use it, follow instructions, small child pads, adult pads fallback) and Red Cross CPR page 23 ("get equipment", continue until an AED is ready). Added as step 13; child pads cited to 19 only because 23 does not mention pads. Adult-pad fallback left in unverified.
- R6 strong cough with trouble breathing: UMass 20 raw confirmed ("trouble breathing but can talk and has a strong cough: Call 911"). Added to step 1 and callEmergencyNow. Also corrected earlier wording that UMass says call for any strong cough.
- R7: (a) wrong hospital name removed everywhere; (b,c) coins, batteries, balloons now cite AAP 10, magnets 17 and 22 (CDC raw-confirmed by WebFetch to mention none of them); (d) 15 added for "do not wait for symptoms"; (e) SJA UK 5 and Healthy WA 9 added to call-timing disagreement; (f) SickKids 11 added to two-finger side, MedlinePlus "palm" noted; (g) 11 added for "well below the breastbone"; (h) call-911 triggers attributed to the guide, not AAP.
- R8: 1-3 year food wording tightened (hot dogs and grapes only if chopped tiny, AAP). Popcorn re-sourced to SickKids 11 and CDC 13 because AAP does not list it. SickKids lengthwise slicing noted as less cautious than AAP tiny pieces.
- R9: disclaimer field added to JSON; bookkeeping lines moved to changeLog; battery in nose or ear added (poison.org 14, 15, with no ear or nose drops from 14); coin line limited to Alder Hey; WebMD Ignite licensing noted for 19 and 20; NHS lap position moved into the disagreement text.

- R1 child too-large or pregnant "concrete hand position": declined. Only Healthy WA gives a position, and for its general method, not this special case; Red Cross gives none. Rule needs two authorities, so the step is marked needs clinician or instructor and the position sits in Disagreements.
- R5 "child pads [19, 23]": narrowed to [19]; the Red Cross CPR page does not mention pads.
- R3 "Do not make the child vomit" was cited to poison.org 14 in rev 2; raw 14 does not say it. Now cited to Alder Hey 16 only.
- Extra finding: SickKids "do not hit the back" is stated for a child who can answer and cough, so the separate "striking a responsive child" contradiction was overstated and merged into the child-technique disagreement.
- Extra finding: Healthy WA and St John NSW infant pages hold the mouth open and (WA) remove loose material with the little finger; added to the mouth-check disagreement.
