## 2. "Much more": beyond the prototype

The owner's decision is "the same, with parity, and much more". This section lists what the real Flutter app already has that the prototype does not (keep all of it; the prototype must not become a ceiling), and what neither has but the audits say a real family product needs. Items marked **DECIDE** need an owner call before they are scheduled; they are never invented here.
