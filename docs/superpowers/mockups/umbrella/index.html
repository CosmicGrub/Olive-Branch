The Olive umbrella

One app, one calm shell, and a shelf of suites underneath. Each family switches on what fits it: a parent far away, two homes, a grandparent raising her, a Deaf child, a long stay in hospital. Every idea here is a proposal, drawn as it could look in the app, with what it builds on, the rules it must keep, and the question still open.
