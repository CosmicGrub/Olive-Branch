Each screen in the order a family meets it. “Rule” lines name the engine function that backs the behaviour, so a screen can’t promise something the logic doesn’t do. G is the guardian app, C is the child app.

Rules and open choices

The child never sees

  • A clock, a countdown, or a number of minutes.
  • A connection or sync meter. A slow link just keeps playing.
  • Words like “allowance”, “limit”, “budget”, “session” or “synced”.
  • Any control that steers the guardian’s view.
  • Who raised or lowered the time.

The child always has

  • One large “All done” button, in the same place on every screen.
  • A calm, wordless pause if the guardian pauses.
  • A gentle “Last one for today” cue before the end.
  • A friendly ending in all three cases: they stopped, the guardian stopped, time ran out.

Choices still open

  • Cap scope. One cap per child across both homes (recommended), or per household.
  • Raising time. Either guardian may; the other is told (recommended).
  • Ceiling. The prototype stops at 8 h. That number is a placeholder.
  • “More” sources. Four candidates are shown on G2; which ones ship is not decided.
  • Phase 3 focus rule. Camera and mic stay on during a share (decided); the written amendment to §5.21.2 is not drafted yet.
